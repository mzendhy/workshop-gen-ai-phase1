**Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** is an AI framework that improves the quality and reliability of Large Language Model (LLM) outputs by grounding them in external, verifiable knowledge sources.

Think of it as the difference between a **closed-book exam** (standard LLM) and an **open-book exam** (RAG).

---

### 1. The Problem: Why do we need RAG?
Standard LLMs have three critical limitations:
*   **Knowledge Cutoff:** They only know data up to their training date.
*   **Hallucinations:** They confidently generate plausible-sounding but false information.
*   **No Private Data Access:** They cannot answer questions about your company’s internal PDFs, proprietary databases, or yesterday’s meeting notes without retraining (which is expensive and slow).

---

### 2. The Core Architecture: Two Main Components
RAG splits the work between a **Retrieval System** (Search) and a **Generative Model** (LLM).

| Component | Role | Analogy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Retriever** | Finds relevant documents/chunks from an external knowledge base based on the user query. | **The Librarian / Research Assistant** |
| **Generator (LLM)** | Synthesizes the retrieved context + the user query into a coherent, natural language answer. | **The Writer / Analyst** |

---
